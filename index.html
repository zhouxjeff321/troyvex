The nation's largest JROTC VEX robotics program.

Eleven cadets started this in a garage three years ago. Today: seven teams, sixty-plus cadets, and the national championship — every robot built by students.

Why it matters

Student-led and school-based: the cadets run this program themselves, out of Room 401. Most walk in with no robotics experience at all — one season later, this is what they build, and where every dollar behind it goes.

#1JROTC robotics program in the country
25+Awards last season, including the national title
1 in 2Cadets arrive with zero experience
100%Of every dollar reaches students
Where they go next

Alumni are studying engineering at some of the strongest programs in the country, and cadets intern alongside them — at JPL at Caltech, the Air Force Research Lab, Stanford, Yale, UChicago and Peraton among them. The technical and communication skills behind those placements are the same ones built on this team.

Join the program

Open to any cadet in Troy's NJROTC unit. We teach building, programming, and strategy from scratch.
