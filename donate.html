Support Troy VEX · Fullerton, CA

Fuel the next generation of engineers.

Troy VEX is 100% funded by sponsors and community supporters. Every dollar buys parts, sends cadets to national competition, and opens a pathway to top universities and STEM careers.

Where it goes

What a gift pays for

01

Parts and fabrication

Every robot on this team is built by students, and every one of them is built from purchased stock. Gifts buy the motors, brains, wheels and metal that a season runs through.

02

Competition travel

Last season took cadets to the JROTC National Championship, the VEX Robotics World Championship, and signature events from Diamond in the Desert to the Lobstah Bowl. Travel is the largest single cost of competing.

03

The next cadet in

More than half the program is freshmen, and one in two cadets arrives with no robotics experience at all. Support is what keeps the door open to them.
