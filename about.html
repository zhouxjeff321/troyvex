Engineering Excellence in the Heart of Fullerton

We are more than just a robotics team. We are a collection of cadets, engineers, and future innovators dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what is possible in the VEX Robotics Competition.

Our story

The Origin Story

Three years ago, a vision took shape within the walls of Troy High School. What began as a modest group of eleven curious cadets has flourished into the nation's premier JROTC VEX Robotics program.

Our foundation is built on the core values of the Navy Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps: honor, courage, and commitment. We integrate these principles with rigorous STEM education, creating an environment where technical skills meet leadership development.

Today, we field seven competitive teams with over 60 dedicated members. Our impact is felt at every level of competition, from garage scrimmages to the National stage.

Timeline

Three chapters

2023 Chapter I

11 cadets, 1 garage. Aaron Li and Kausthubh Veldanda rallied a tight-knit crew. This small, tight-knit family provided the program the values and drive we live by today.

2024 Chapter II

The hard work paid off. In a defining season, the team secured 2 Division Finalist Titles at JROTC Nationals. The program doubled in size to 35.

2025 Chapter III

Led by student leaders Ryan Zhou, Krishna Sivakumar, and Avijeet Singh, the program headed to new heights. 60+ members, 7 teams. 4 World qualifications. The largest and strongest VEX JROTC organization in the nation.

Philosophy

Our Philosophy

01

Disciplined Innovation

Creativity without structure is chaos. We apply military-grade discipline to the engineering design process, ensuring our robots are not only innovative but reliable and rigorously tested.

02

Collective Success

A victory for one team is a victory for the entire program. We foster a culture of open knowledge sharing, where senior members mentor novices, elevating the performance of the entire unit.

03

Future Readiness

We are preparing students for more than just a game. Our cadets gain proficiency in CAD, programming, public speaking, and project management — skills that serve them for a lifetime.
