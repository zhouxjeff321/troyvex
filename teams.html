How a team works

Two cadets, one team

Every team carries the same two roles. The titles come from NJROTC; the work is ordinary robotics.

01

Commanding Officer

The CO answers for the team. They set the build direction, decide what gets built and when, and represent the team at competition.

02

Executive Officer

The XO is second in command and keeps the team running day to day — meetings, the engineering notebook, parts, and the practice schedule.

03

The rest of the team

Everyone else builds, programs, drives, and scouts. Teams run five to twelve cadets, and roles move around as the season goes on.

Above the teams

Program leadership

Three cadets run the program across all nine teams — the schedule, the lab, and the logistics that no single team owns. Commander Lauper coaches.
